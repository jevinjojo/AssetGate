1. Summary & Value Proposition

Real-World Assets (RWAs)—such as private credit funds, commercial real estate, structured debt, and treasuries—cannot be legally represented by standard ERC-20 tokens. Global regulatory frameworks require strict on-chain guarantees:

  • Identity Verification: Only KYC/AML-verified investors may hold or receive the asset.
  • Transfer Restrictions: Asset movement must enforce jurisdictional rules, single-investor concentration caps, and max holder thresholds.
  • Institutional Governance: Issuers require legal capabilities to freeze malicious wallets, pause trading during halts, execute court-ordered asset recovery, and distribute automated pro-rata dividends.

AssetGate brings institutional-grade RWA infrastructure to BOT Chain. Built on the battle-tested ERC-3643 (T-REX) standard, it establishes a turnkey B2B platform with on-chain compliance gates, resilient PostgreSQL event indexing, zero-gas preflight simulations, and specialized Issuer/Investor portals.

2. Smart Contract Architecture (The Two-Gate Model)

To meet global security token regulations, standard ERC-20 transfers are strictly forbidden. In AssetGate, every asset movement (transfer, transferFrom, mint) must pass through two independent, sequential on-chain verification gates before balances can update:

       Sender (Verified) ────────── transfer(to, amount) ──────────► Recipient
                                       │
                                       ▼
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  GATE 1: Identity Registry (IdentityRegistry.sol)           │
        │  • Is recipient registered in the Identity Registry?        │
        │  • Does recipient hold a valid ONCHAINID contract?          │
        │  • Does ONCHAINID hold a valid KYC Claim (Topic 1)          │
        │    cryptographically signed by a Trusted Claim Issuer?      │
        └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                       │
                               [Passes Gate 1]
                                       │
                                       ▼
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  GATE 2: Modular Compliance (ModularCompliance.sol)         │
        │  • Country Restriction: Is recipient's country allowed?     │
        │  • Balance Cap: Does amount exceed single-wallet limit?     │
        │  • Holder Threshold: Does transfer exceed max holder cap?   │
        └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                       │
                         ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
                         ▼                           ▼
                 [Both Gates Pass]            [Either Fails]
                         │                           │
                   Tokens Move                 On-Chain Revert
               Event Emitted & Indexed         Zero Balance Change

Core Architectural Guarantees:

  • Gate 1 (Identity & Attestation): Verifies investor eligibility via decentralized ONCHAINID (ERC-734/735). The token queries IdentityRegistry.isVerified(recipient), confirming that an authorized claim issuer signed their KYC claim off-chain.
  • Gate 2 (Modular Compliance Rules): Evaluates dynamic jurisdictional rules via ModularCompliance.canTransfer(). If the transfer breaches country restrictions, holding limits, or maximum investor caps, execution halts immediately.
  • Strict Atomicity: If either gate returns false, the transaction reverts on-chain with zero state change. Only when both gates pass are balances debited/credited and indexed by our real-time engine.

3. Core Smart Contracts & Compliance Modules

All smart contracts below have been compiled via Foundry, orchestrated with DeployAll.s.sol, and deployed on BOT Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 677), with core contracts verified on-chain and auxiliary modules pending explorer source verification (see Section 9 for status breakdown):

Contract Architectural Responsibility
Token.sol (AGT) Permissioned ERC-3643 asset token. Overrides transfers to enforce Gate 1 + 2 and holds administrative controls (mint, burn, freeze, pause, recovery).
IdentityRegistry.sol On-chain registry mapping investor wallets to their ONCHAINID contract and country code. Answers isVerified(address).
IdentityRegistryStorage.sol Decoupled storage contract separating identity data from execution logic, allowing future logic upgrades without migrating investor state.
ModularCompliance.sol Compliance rule engine. Binds active modules and dynamically verifies transfers via canTransfer(from, to, amount).
ClaimTopicsRegistry.sol Registry of mandatory verification topics. Configured for Topic 1 = KYC.
TrustedIssuersRegistry.sol Whitelist of authorized identity attestation providers, preventing forged or self-signed claims.
ClaimIssuer.sol Trusted ONCHAINID claim signer contract holding authorized ECDSA public keys for KYC attestations.

Active Compliance Modules:

  • CountryRestrictModule.sol: Enforces investor jurisdiction eligibility (e.g., permitting ISO 840 US investors while blocking sanctioned nations).
  • BalanceCapModule.sol: Prevents token concentration by capping maximum holdings per single investor.
  • MaxHolderCountModule.sol: Automatically enforces private placement limits (e.g. maximum 500 holders).

4. ONCHAINID Privacy & Cryptographic Attestation

We designed the identity layer to guarantee institutional compliance without exposing sensitive investor data on a public ledger:

  • Decoupled Identity Contracts: Every investor has a dedicated ONCHAINID smart contract (ERC-734 / ERC-735), separating their on-chain legal persona from their transient wallet keys.
  • Zero PII On-Chain: Passports, tax IDs, and personal documents never touch BOT Chain. The blockchain records only cryptographic attestations.
  • ECDSA Claim Signatures: Our trusted Claim Issuer signs an off-chain attestation hash: keccak256(identityAddress, TOPIC_KYC, "PASSED_KYC"). The smart contract validates only this signature, keeping verification instantaneous and gas-minimal.

5. Resilient Indexing Engine & Database Fault-Tolerance

AssetGate maintains a live, zero-latency Cap Table backed by Fastify, Prisma ORM, and Neon PostgreSQL without overloading BOT Chain's RPC nodes:

  • Conservative 100-Block Batches: Configured with BATCH_SIZE = 100 against https://rpc.botchain.ai as recommended by the BOT Chain engineering team, eliminating RPC timeouts.
  • Atomic ACID Checkpoints: Checkpoint updates (lastBlock) are committed inside prisma.$transaction alongside event inserts.
  • Automated Fault-Tolerance Tests: Verification tests prove that RPC failures or database deadlocks immediately roll back and leave lastBlock strictly unchanged. On restart, the indexer resumes from lastBlock + 1 with zero duplicate records (enforced by txHash_logIndex unique keys).

6. Off-Chain Preflight Simulation Shield

On public blockchains, compliance failures revert on-chain and burn real gas fees. AssetGate eliminates this friction with its Preflight Shield (/api/v1/transfer-preflight):

Before MetaMask is prompted, our backend runs an off-chain compliance simulation in under 15ms. If the recipient is unverified or exceeds a compliance cap, the UI immediately displays ⛔ The recipient is not a verified investor and halts execution, saving 100% of failed gas fees.

8. Role-Based Access Control & Dashboard Agent Manager

To ensure high institutional security while making corporate administration frictionless, AssetGate combines strict smart contract RBAC with an interactive dashboard permission manager:

  • On-Chain Security: Administrative controls (mint, freeze, forcedTransfer, pause) strictly require the AGENT_ROLE on both the Token and Identity Registry contracts. Unauthorized calls revert immediately.
  • Interactive UI Agent Manager: Contract owners and compliance officers can delegate or revoke the Agent role directly through the governance interface without executing manual command-line scripts: 
    // Operator permissions can be granted or revoked directly from the web UI:
token.addAgent(operatorAddress);
identityRegistry.addAgent(operatorAddress);
    Simply input the target operator's wallet address, click Grant agent, and that account is immediately authorized on-chain to execute minting, compliance freezes, and emergency halts.
  • Instant Transaction Proofs: Confirmation banners on all dashboard controls render live receipt links directly to BOT Chain Explorer, allowing compliance officers and auditors to verify the underlying state change on-chain in real time.
